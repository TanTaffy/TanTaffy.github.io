# About me  
I am an engineer based in Manchester currently enrolled in the University of Manchester, a Powertrains Engineer for the University of Manchester Eco-Racing Team and a huge tech enthusiast for a majority of my life!

A keen learner since young, I've tried my hand on a variety of different projects, particularly to do with the automotive industry, where I've held close as long as I remember, but studying at University has broadened my horizons to push myself to become the person I am today.

## Experience & skills
* **Embedded systems & Microcontrollers** Using microcontrollers such as Raspberry Pis and Arduino boards to bridge the gap between software and hardware to build efficient, responsive real-time systems. 
* **Circuit design & analysis** Power systems, signal processing and fundamental PCB designs.
* **Software-Hardware Integration** Leveraging languages such as Python/C++ as well as using programs such as Simhub in order to control hardware peripherals, automate tests and process data received from sensors.

## Technical Stack
* **Programming** Python, C/C++,  HTML, MATLAB
* **Tools** VS Code, Linux/Terminal, MATLAB, Fusion 360, KiCad, Notion
